- Dustin Crummett, ["Arguments from Silence are Often Good,"](https://dustincrummett261574.substack.com/p/arguments-from-silence-are-often) Substack (2026). An argument from silence is the inference that because something wasn't mentioned, it probably didn't happen. Crummett defends this form of reasoning against the objection that our intuitions about what an author would have mentioned are unreliable, arguing that with attention to an author's purposes and circumstances, those intuitions hold up well.
