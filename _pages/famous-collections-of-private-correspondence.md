- *The Paston Letters* (1422–1509). Correspondence of the Paston family of Norfolk. Edited by James Gairdner; complete library edition in six volumes (Archibald Constable, 1904). Discussed in Virginia Woolf, "The Pastons and Chaucer."

- Lord Chesterfield, [*Letters to His Son*](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/3361/3361-h/3361-h.htm) (1746–1771).

- George Sand and Gustave Flaubert, [*The George Sand–Gustave Flaubert Letters*](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/5115) (1863–1876), trans. Aimée L. McKenzie (Boni and Liveright, 1921). Quoted in [Writer’s block](/treasury/writers-block/).
