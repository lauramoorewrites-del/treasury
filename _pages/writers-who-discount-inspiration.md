- **Marlon James.** "When people say they write when they're inspired, I'm like, oh my God, I haven't been inspired to write something since 1988. I sit down and do the work because I got to eat. If I were to wait for inspiration, I don't know what would happen. I will say this though. I think if you set a practice or a routine or a work ethic, whatever you call it, the muses will show up. I think muses have to be convinced that you're serious." — Interviewed by Shandela Contreras, ["For Marlon James, Being Incurious Is a Writer's Biggest Sin,"](https://electricliterature.com/for-marlon-james-being-incurious-is-a-writers-biggest-sin/) *Electric Literature* (2026).
