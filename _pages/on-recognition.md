> To recognize something is to be affected by what we already know but didn’t realize, an insight that leads us back to what is both familiar and strange.

— Jeff Porter, [“The History and Poetics of the Essay,”](https://www.assayjournal.com/jeff-porter-the-history-and-poetics-of-the-essay-assay-121.html) *Assay: A Journal of Nonfiction Studies* 12.1; adapted from Patricia Foster and Jeff Porter, eds., *Understanding the Essay* (Broadview Press, 2012).
